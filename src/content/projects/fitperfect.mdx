## Summary

FitPerfect is a private-by-default iOS MVP that turns exercise video into local form
feedback: rep counts, joint-angle analysis, and 3D motion data for squats and overhead
presses. Built solo, April 2024 – May 2025.

## Context and constraints

All inference runs on-device — no video or pose data leaves the phone. That constraint drove
every downstream decision: model size, runtime, and camera calibration all had to work within
a mobile ONNX Runtime budget rather than a server GPU.

## Approach

Hand-built the ML pipeline: YOLOv8 for person detection, RTMPose for 2D keypoints, a
COCO-to-H36M conversion step, and MotionBERT for temporal 3D pose lifting. Engineered
coordinate transforms and platform-aware ONNX inference so skeleton overlays stay aligned
with the camera feed under real capture conditions (varying angles, distances, lighting).
AI-assisted development was used for the Flutter mobile implementation, while the ML pipeline
itself was hand-built.

## Evaluation

Evaluated primarily through direct capture testing across squat and overhead-press form,
checking overlay alignment and joint-angle accuracy against real camera conditions rather
than a held-out benchmark dataset.

## Result

A working iOS MVP that produces local rep counts, joint-angle analysis, and 3D motion data
in real time, with no cloud dependency.

## What changed

Camera calibration and coordinate-transform work took longer than expected — getting overlays
to stay visually aligned across different phones and capture angles was the hardest part of
the project, more so than the pose-estimation models themselves.
