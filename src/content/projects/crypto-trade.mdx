## Summary

Crypto Trade is an event-driven research platform that ingests PumpPortal migration events and
collects time-stamped on-chain, market, holder, quote, risk, and alternative-data evidence for
each one. Built solo, May–July 2026.

## Context and constraints

Migration events fire in bursts and the source data comes from several independent feeds, so
the system had to handle concurrent writes, partial failures, and rate limits without losing
or duplicating records — a requirement for the data to be usable in downstream research at all.

## Approach

Designed idempotent concurrent workers, a SQLite WAL-based lifecycle tracker per event,
configurable risk gates and rate limits, and append-only Zstandard-compressed Parquet records
so every capture is auditable after the fact.

## Evaluation

Validated primarily by auditing captured records for completeness and consistency across
concurrent worker runs, rather than a formal benchmark — the goal was reliable data capture,
not a predictive model.

## Result

A working pipeline producing auditable, append-only, time-stamped evidence per migration event
for later research use.

## What changed

Idempotency and lifecycle tracking under concurrency needed more design work than the initial
data-collection logic — worth planning for up front on any similar event-driven ingestion
project.
